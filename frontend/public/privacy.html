Locuvi privacy policy

Locuvi is free, open-source software for GP locums to keep their diary, invoices, payments and pension forms. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Your records stay with you

Locuvi runs on your own devices: your computer, phone or tablet, or a server you control. There is no Locuvi account and no Locuvi company that receives, stores or sees your records. Locuvi has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you switch a feature on, and then only what that feature needs:

  • Sync sends your records and files, encrypted on your device with your sync code, to the place you choose: a cloud folder, your Nextcloud, or your Dropbox or Google Drive account.
  • Email sends an invoice through your own email provider when you tap Send.
  • The AI assistant (optional) sends booking text, with personal details removed, to the AI company you choose.
  • Updates: the apps ask GitHub whether there is a new version. Nothing about you is sent.

Dropbox and Google Drive

If you choose to sync through your Dropbox or Google Drive account, Locuvi asks for the smallest access those services offer:

  • Dropbox: only Locuvi's own folder inside Dropbox's Apps folder, and your account's email address to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive: only the files Locuvi itself creates, in a folder called Locuvi Sync (the drive.file permission). Locuvi can't see your other files. Your email address is shown to say which account is connected.

Everything Locuvi puts there is encrypted first, so Dropbox and Google see only file names and sizes, never your records. The sign-in is kept encrypted on your device and is never sent anywhere else.

Locuvi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is used only to sync your own records between your own devices. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone.

You can disconnect at any time: switch sync off in Locuvi, and remove Locuvi's access in your Dropbox or Google account settings. Deleting the Locuvi Sync folder deletes everything Locuvi put there.

Questions

The full privacy guide is in Locuvi's documentation. Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
