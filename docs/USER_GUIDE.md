## 👋 Your first day

Open Locuvi in your web browser using the address the person who set it up gave you. It works
on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like
an app (in Safari: **Share › Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome: **⋮ › Add to Home screen**).

<img src="images/sign-in.png" width="420" alt="The sign-in screen with name and password boxes">

Sign in with your password. If more than one person in your household uses Locuvi, type your
name as well.

The first time, Locuvi asks three short questions:

| Step | What it asks | Why |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Your details**: your name and address | They appear at the top of every invoice |
| 2 | **Where practices should pay you**: your bank details | Printed on invoices so practices can pay you |
| 3 | **Your first practice**: name, address and usual fee | So Locuvi can fill in invoices for you |

That's it. You can change any of these later in **Settings**.

> [!TIP]
> Locuvi saves as you type. There is no "Save" button to forget, and most actions have an
> **Undo** button instead of an "Are you sure?" question.

## 🗓 A normal month

Most months take a few taps:

```
 Diary            Today              Invoice             Paid               Pension
 ─────            ─────              ───────             ────               ───────
 Tap the days  →  "You can invoice → Check it, then  →  Record the      →  Copy the figures
 you worked       for September"     tap Issue          payment            onto Form A and B
```

1. **Diary**: tap each day you work.
2. **Today**: when a month is finished, Locuvi says *"You can invoice for September"*. Tap
   **Create invoice**.
3. **Invoice**: check the draft, then tap **Issue invoice and download**. Email it to the
   practice with **Send**, or download the PDF.
4. **Getting paid**: when the money arrives, record it with **Record as paid**, or upload a bank
   statement and let Locuvi match payments for you.
5. **Pension**: after the month ends, Locuvi shows the figures for your NHS pension forms.

## 🏠 Today

The **Today** page is your home screen. It shows what needs doing, one tap each.

<img src="images/today.png" alt="Today page: totals, a message that nothing needs attention, things to check and upcoming bookings">

- **The three boxes at the top** show what you invoiced this month, what you are still owed
  and your total for the tax year.
- **Things that need you** appear in the middle: a month ready to invoice, an invoice that's
  late, a pension form due. Each has a button that takes you straight there.
- **Things to check** are gentle reminders, such as backups or a missing setting. Tap **Hide**
  if you don't need one.
- **Your next bookings** lists the next two weeks of work.

The **+** button (bottom right) adds work quickly from any page.

## 📅 Diary

The Diary is where you record the sessions you work.

<img src="images/diary.png" alt="Diary page: choose a practice and session type, then tap days on the calendar">

1. Choose the **practice** at the top (tap **+** to add a new one).
2. Choose the **type of work**: **AM**, **PM**, **Full day** or **Hours**.
3. **Tap each day** you work. A coloured label appears on the day.

Locuvi remembers each practice's fee for each type of work, shown on the right
(for example **£640.00 ✎**). Tap it to change the fee.

- **Change or remove a session**: tap the label on the calendar.
- **Regular work**: tap **Add repeating sessions…**, for example every Tuesday morning until
  Christmas.
- **The box under the calendar** shows each practice's total for the month and whether it has
  been invoiced.

### Adding work from a booking email

Copy the text of a booking email, tap **Add bookings from a message**, paste it in and tap
**Find work dates**. Locuvi lists the sessions it found, so you can check them before anything
is added.

<img src="images/booking-review.png" alt="Check the sessions before adding them: two sessions, each marked 'This is already in your diary'">

Locuvi points out anything unusual, such as *This is already in your diary*, a clash with
another practice, or a cancellation. Nothing changes until you tap **Add sessions**.

## 🧾 Invoices

### Creating an invoice

When a month is finished, tap **Create invoice** on **Today** or under the Diary calendar.
Locuvi fills in the draft for you: practice, dates, fees and the practice's NHS pension
contribution.

On the right, **What the practice will pay** adds it all up. Drafts save themselves.

- **View PDF before issuing** shows exactly what the practice will receive.
- **Issue invoice and download** gives the invoice its number (for example `LOC-2026-0001`)
  and creates the final PDF.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Once issued, an invoice can't be changed. This keeps your records honest and is what
> accountants and HMRC expect. If something is wrong, you can **Cancel invoice**, or
> **Correct with a credit note** if the practice has already recorded it.

Here is what an invoice looks like (made-up details). You can add your logo and colour in
**Settings › Invoice appearance**.

<img src="images/example-invoice.png" width="560" alt="An example A4 invoice from Dr Alex Example to Example Medical Practice">

### Your invoices

<img src="images/invoices.png" alt="The Invoices list with filters for drafts, unpaid and paid invoices">

The **Invoices** page lists every invoice. Use **Drafts**, **Unpaid** and **Paid** to filter,
or search by number, practice or reference. Late invoices are shown first on **Today**.

Open an invoice to see everything about it:

<img src="images/invoice.png" alt="An issued invoice showing amount, Paid status, the work lines, payments, a credit note and linked emails">

- **PDF** downloads it again, at any time.
- **Send** emails it to the practice from your own email address (set up once in
  **Settings › Email invoices**). Locuvi never sends anything unless you tap Send.
- **Record as paid** appears while the invoice is unpaid.
- **More** has: correct with a credit note, make a new draft from this, and cancel.
- **Payments received**, **credit notes** and **emails and payment documents** linked to the
  invoice are listed underneath.

### Credit notes (corrections)

If a practice has already recorded your invoice and something needs to come off it, such as a
cancelled session, open the invoice, tap **More › Correct with a credit note**, tick the lines
and give a reason. The credit note gets its own number (`CN-0001`) and PDF. If the invoice
was already paid, Locuvi reminds you that a refund is due.

## 💷 Getting paid

When money arrives, open the invoice (or tap it on **Today**) and tap **Record as paid**. Made
a mistake? Tap **Undo**.

Or let Locuvi do the matching: on **Invoices**, tap **Check bank payments** and
**Upload a bank statement** (the CSV file from your online banking). Locuvi:

- keeps **only money coming in**. Your spending is ignored and never saved.
- suggests which payment belongs to which invoice.
- records nothing until you tap **Record suggested payments**.

## 🏛 NHS pension

The **Pension** page prepares the figures for your monthly GP locum pension forms. Locuvi
**does not submit** anything: you copy the figures onto the official NHS forms.

<img src="images/pension-month.png" alt="A month's pension figures: Form A for each practice and Form B for money received">

For each month you'll see:

- **Form A** (one per practice, per month worked): dates, fees, pensionable pay, the
  practice's contribution and the **10-week deadline**. Track it with
  **Not sent → Sent, waiting for signature → Signed by practice**.
- **Form B** (money received that month): the totals you send with your own contribution,
  due by the **7th of the next month**.
- **Download figures** gives you a worksheet PDF to work from.
- **I've submitted Form B** records that you've done it.

Later, you can type in (or **Import PCSE figures**) what your NHS pension record shows.
Locuvi tells you if anything doesn't match.

> [!TIP]
> Locuvi works out the practice's contribution as **14.38% of 90% of your NHS fees**. For
> example, £600 → pensionable pay £540 → contribution £77.65 → invoice total £677.65. If the
> rate changes, add the new rate in **Settings › NHS pension**.

## 📊 Reports and your tax return

<img src="images/reports.png" alt="Reports: tax-year totals, a monthly chart, totals by practice and downloads for your accountant">

**Reports** shows your tax year (6 April to 5 April) at a glance: money invoiced, received and
still owed, month by month and practice by practice.

- **Date you were paid** or **Date you invoiced**: choose the one your accountant uses. Most
  self-employed GPs use the date paid.
- **Figures for your tax return** separates your fees from the practices' pension
  contributions.
- **Downloads for your accountant**: an invoice list and a payments list (spreadsheets), or
  **All documents (ZIP)** with every invoice PDF for the year.

## 📨 Booking emails and the AI assistant

### The Inbox

The tray icon (top right) opens your **Inbox**. Drop in anything to do with work: booking
emails, payment advice, bank statements, pension letters (PDF, photo, email file or pasted
text). Mark each one **Done** when you've dealt with it.

<img src="images/inbox.png" alt="The Inbox, empty, with buttons to upload a file or add bookings from a message">

### Reading your email for bookings (optional)

Locuvi can check chosen email folders, such as a "Locum" folder, for booking emails and
payment notices. It **only reads**: it never sends, moves, deletes or marks anything as read.
Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365, iCloud and most others work, and you can add several accounts.

See **[Connecting your email](EMAIL_SETUP.md)** for step-by-step instructions.

### AI assistant (optional, off unless you turn it on)

Locuvi reads most simple booking emails by itself. If you'd like help with messy emails,
scans and photos, you can turn on an AI assistant from the company of your choice: Anthropic
(Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI (Grok) or OpenRouter.

With the assistant on, the ✨ icon opens **Ask Locuvi**, where you can ask things like
*"Which practices still owe me money?"* or *"Prepare invoices for last month"*. It can look
things up and prepare drafts, but it can **never** issue an invoice, send an email, record a
payment or submit a pension form. You always do those yourself.

See **[Setting up the AI assistant](AI_SETUP.md)**.

## 🔧 Settings

Tap the gear icon (top right).

<img src="images/settings.png" width="640" alt="The Settings list">

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Your details**, **Bank account** | What's printed on your invoices |
| **Invoice appearance** | Add your logo, choose a colour, and add a short message or email sign-off |
| **Practices** | Addresses, usual fees and invoicing notes for each practice |
| **Invoice settings** | How invoice numbers start, how many days practices have to pay, VAT |
| **NHS pension** | The pension rates Locuvi uses, and your own contribution rate |
| **Email invoices** | Send invoices from your own email address |
| **Nextcloud copies** | Keep an extra copy of each invoice PDF in your Nextcloud |
| **Phone or computer calendar** | See your booked sessions in your usual calendar app (**Add to my calendar**) |
| **Booking emails** | Let Locuvi look for bookings in chosen email folders |
| **Assistant** | Turn on the optional AI assistant |
| **Connect another AI assistant** | Let Bevro or another AI agent look up your records (read-only unless you allow drafts) |
| **Recently deleted** | Bring back anything deleted in the last 30 days |
| **Password** | Change your password |
| **Profiles** | *(first user only)* Let others in your household use Locuvi, each with their own private data |

### Making invoices look like yours

<img src="images/settings-personalisation.png" alt="Invoice appearance: logo, accent colour, footer message and a sample preview">

### Recently deleted

Deleted something by mistake? Sessions, draft invoices, payments, inbox documents, email
accounts and pension settings stay in
**Settings › Recently deleted** for 30 days. Tap **Restore** to bring them back.
Issued invoices are never deleted.

<img src="images/settings-trash.png" alt="The Recently deleted list with Restore buttons">

### Household profiles

The first person to use Locuvi is the **admin**. In **Settings › Profiles** they can
**Add a profile** for someone else in the household. Each person gets their own password and
their own completely separate diary, practices, invoices, pension figures and backups. No one
can see anyone else's data.

<img src="images/settings-profiles.png" alt="The Profiles page listing the household members">

A removed profile can be restored for 30 days. After that, its data is deleted for good.

## ❓ Common questions

<details>
<summary><b>I made a mistake on an invoice I already issued</b></summary>

Issued invoices can't be edited. Open it and tap **More**:
- **Cancel invoice** if the practice hasn't recorded it yet, then **Make a new draft from this**,
  correct it and issue the new one.
- **Correct with a credit note** if the practice has already recorded or paid it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I deleted something by mistake</b></summary>

Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen, or go to
**Settings › Recently deleted** within 30 days.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I forgot my password</b></summary>

If someone else in your household is the admin, ask them to set a new password for you in
**Settings › Profiles**. If you are the admin, the person who looks after the server can reset
it (see [Setting up Locuvi](SETUP.md#forgotten-password)).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Locuvi submit my pension forms or send emails by itself?</b></summary>

No. Locuvi prepares figures and drafts. Sending an email, submitting a form or recording a
payment always needs your tap.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is my data safe?</b></summary>

Your data lives on your own server, not with a company. Locuvi backs itself up every night,
and can keep an encrypted copy somewhere else (see [Setting up Locuvi](SETUP.md#backups)).
Passwords and keys are stored encrypted and are never shown again after you save them.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is the pension rate right?</b></summary>

Locuvi comes with the 2026/27 employer rate (14.38%). Rates change. Always check them against
current NHS Business Services Authority and PCSE guidance, and add new rates in
**Settings › NHS pension**.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use Locuvi if I'm VAT registered?</b></summary>

Not yet. Locuvi doesn't produce VAT invoices. Most GP medical services are VAT-exempt, but ask
your accountant if you're unsure.
</details>

## 📖 Words explained

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Session** | A block of work: a morning (AM), an afternoon (PM), a full day, or a number of hours |
| **Issue** | Turn a draft into a final, numbered invoice with a PDF. It can't be changed afterwards |
| **Credit note** | A document that reduces an invoice that's already been issued, instead of editing it |
| **Pensionable pay** | The part of your NHS fees that counts for your NHS pension: 90% of the fee |
| **Employer contribution** | What the practice pays into your NHS pension on top of your fee (currently 14.38% of pensionable pay) |
| **Form A** | The NHS locum form for the work you did at one practice in one month; the practice signs it |
| **Form B** | The monthly NHS locum form for the money you received, sent with your contributions |
| **PCSE** | Primary Care Support England, which runs NHS pension administration for GPs |
| **Tax year** | 6 April to 5 April, the year HMRC uses |
| **CSV** | A simple spreadsheet file. Your bank and Excel can both open and save them |
| **API key** | A password-like code from an AI company that lets Locuvi use their AI on your account |
| **Nextcloud** | A private cloud storage service (like Dropbox) that you can run yourself |
