# Locuvi brand — concise identity

**Name:** Locuvi (stylised lowercase **locuvi** in wordmark; title-case in body). Internal personal software, not for distribution. No trademark/domain work needed for MVP.

**Tagline:** *Every session accounted for.*  
**v0.1 descriptor:** GP Locum Invoicing  
**Later product descriptor:** GP Locum Management

**Tone:** Calm, precise, professional, quietly reassuring; no playful healthcare cartoons. Visually related to Pistio's restrained steel-blue/burgundy world but independent.

**Palette**
- Steel blue `#294D63` — principal brand/action
- Deep ink `#203847` — headings
- Muted burgundy `#8D5968` — rare secondary emphasis (not destructive action)
- Cloud `#F2F5F6` — light neutral backgrounds
- White `#FFFFFF` — invoice and cards
- Mint accent `#8AC4BF` — occasional check mark, not substitute for WCAG-compliant textual status

**Type:** Manrope SemiBold for headings/wordmark; Inter for form text, item tables, invoice body. Use distributable package fonts appropriately, and fallbacks. Avoid system dependence for PDF rendering: embed a legally usable font in the build when necessary (do not redistribute unlicensed fonts).

**Mark:** Simple geometric `L`/ledger corner, subtly combined with a small verification tick. Keep at 16–24px legible. Do not use a medical cross, stethoscope, pound sign, robot or AI symbol. Do not rely on icon colour as sole meaning.

**Invoice output:** More neutral than application branding: white A4, dark text, optional narrow steel-blue rule, no needless decoration. No mandatory logo on invoices. PDF's primary purpose is clean accounting communication.

**Assets:** The handover includes a minimal draft SVG mark only, not a final registered logo. Claude may refine it slightly but should prioritise functionality.
